Mahjong Zen

A mahjong solitaire with one twist: the tiles you pick go into a hand of four, and pairs clear themselves there. Take the tile you want and you open three more. Take the wrong one and it sits there, waiting for a partner buried six tiles deep.

  • 180 boards generated in-house, ordered by measured difficulty — every one verified solvable.
  • A board built for the phone, authored for a tall screen rather than shrunk to fit.

Tasty Tiles

A cheerful food-themed triple-match puzzle. Tap any uncovered tile and build sets of three in a seven-slot tray. There is no timer: the tension comes from reading the layered board, opening the right path, and keeping those seven little spaces under control.

  • 100 campaign boards with deterministic layouts and a verified legal winning route.
  • Centered portrait-style playfield inside a full 16:9 canvas, with readable food silhouettes and touch-first controls.

Rights and provenance

For Mahjong Zen: nothing in it is licensed from another game. Code, level design, art and audio were all made for it — written, generated or synthesised in-house — and we own or hold commercial rights to every piece that ships.

Art
Generated on a paid ChatGPT account, whose terms assign the output to us for commercial use.
Music
Generated on a paid-tier Suno account, which assigns all rights in the output to us — commercial use, no attribution required.
Voice
Rendered on a paid-tier ElevenLabs account, where we retain all rights in the output. Its synthetic origin is disclosed in-game, as those terms require.

We keep a written licence register behind that claim: for each asset, the source, the licence text, the account tier it was made under, the date, and a checksum of the exact bytes in the build. Candidates that failed a licence check were rejected rather than shipped, and the reasons are logged too. The register is available to a partner on request, along with a dedicated build with your SDK integrated.
